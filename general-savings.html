General Savings Account (S1)

The primary savings foundation for every NSLS member.

Purpose & Overview

The General Savings Account (S1) is the core account opened when you first join Nambawan Savings & Loan Society. It is designed to meet your short-term financial needs while acting as security for your loans.

Contributions & Fees

  • Membership Fee: A one-off fee of K50.00 is required upon joining.
  • Minimum Contribution: Maintain a minimum fortnightly contribution of K50.00.
  • Loan Security: Your accumulated savings serve as collateral for credit facilities.

Withdrawal Guidelines

We provide flexibility while ensuring your savings continue to grow:

  • Access: Funds are accessible after three (3) months of continuous contributions.
  • Limit: Members can withdraw up to 50% of their net total savings.
  • Frequency: One withdrawal is permitted per month (K20.00 exit fee applies).
  • Minimum Amount: The minimum withdrawal amount is K200.00.

About Us

Nambawan Savings & Loan Society (NSLS) is a newly established Savings & Loan Society under the Savings & Loans Societies (Amended) Act 1995. Nambawan Savings & Loan Society was established to serve the short term financial needs of the Nambawan Super Fund members.
