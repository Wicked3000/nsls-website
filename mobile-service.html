Mobile Banking Service

Manage your NSLS accounts anytime, anywhere, directly from your mobile phone.

Banking at Your Fingertips

Convenience for our members is a priority. The NSLS Mobile Service allows you to access your account information and perform transactions without visiting a branch.

How to Access

Simply dial the USSD code corresponding to your network provider:

For BMobile Users:

*662*MemberNumber*PIN#

For Digicel Users:

*663*MembershipNumber*PIN#

Key Features

  • Balance Inquiry: View real-time balances for all your savings accounts.
  • Eligibility Check: Instantly find out how much you can borrow or withdraw.
  • Transaction Requests: Apply for loans or request savings withdrawals directly through the menu.
  • Secure Access: Protected by a personal security PIN assigned to your membership.

* Note: a session charge of 0.20t applies (levied by the network provider).

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the NSL Mobile service?

Simply dial the USSD code for your network: *662*MemberNumber*PIN# for BMobile or *663*MembershipNumber*PIN# for Digicel.

What are the services available on the NSLS Mobile service?

Members can perform balance inquiries, check loan and withdrawal eligibility, request savings withdrawals, and apply for loans directly from their handset.

Which mobile telecommunication operator offers this service?

The service is currently available to both BMobile and Digicel subscribers across Papua New Guinea.

What types of mobile phones can be used to access NSLS Mobile service?

Any mobile phone that supports USSD technology can be used, ranging from basic "feature" phones to the latest smartphones.

Can I access this NSLS Mobile service anywhere in Papua New Guinea?

Yes, the service is accessible anywhere in PNG as long as there is a mobile network connection from BMobile or Digicel.

Is there a charge for using the NSLS Mobile service?

A small session fee of 0.20t is charged by the network provider (BMobile/Digicel) for each session, not by NSLS.

After receiving "Loan is awaiting Approval" message what should I do?

Your application has been successfully submitted to our system. Please allow 24-48 hours for processing. You will receive an SMS notification once your loan is approved or if further information is required.

Is the Service tied down to the SIM (For Security purposes)?

No, for security, the service is tied to your Membership Number and your unique PIN. This allows you to access your account from any phone as long as you have your credentials.

What happens if two members having different member ID numbers have the same PIN?

The system distinguishes accounts based on the unique combination of Member ID and PIN. However, we highly recommend that you choose a unique PIN that is not easily guessed to enhance your account security.
