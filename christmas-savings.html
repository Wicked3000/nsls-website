Christmas Savings (S3)

Plan ahead for a festive season without the financial stress.

Festive Financial Peace of Mind

The Christmas Savings account (S3) is a specialized product designed to help members save specifically for year-end expenses. Whether it's travel, gifts, or hosting family, S3 ensures you have the target funds ready by December.

How It Works

  • Flexible Contributions: Start with as little as K10.00 fortnightly.
  • Purpose-Driven: Keeps your festive funds separate from your daily banking and general savings.
  • Ease of Use: Automatic payroll deductions make saving effortless.

Withdrawal Terms

To maximize your savings for the holidays, funds are locked until the festive season begins:

  • Availability: Funds are only accessible between **November and January**.
  • Process: Simply submit your withdrawal request to your local NSLS branch during the open period.

About Us

Nambawan Savings & Loan Society (NSLS) is a newly established Savings & Loan Society under the Savings & Loans Societies (Amended) Act 1995. Nambawan Savings & Loan Society was established to serve the short term financial needs of the Nambawan Super Fund members.
