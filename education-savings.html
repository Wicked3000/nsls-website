Education Savings (S2)

Invest in your future with the School Fee Saver Account.

Secure Your Education

The School Fee Saver Account (S2) is specifically designed to help individuals save for educational expenses, whether for themselves or their children. This disciplined savings approach ensures funds are ready when enrollment season arrives.

Account Features

  • Low Barrier Entry: Start saving with a minimum fortnightly contribution of just K10.00.
  • Targeted Purpose: Dedicated strictly to assisting members with educational costs.
  • Disciplined Growth: Regular deductions ensure you consistently work towards your schooling goals.

Accessing Your Funds

To ensure funds are used for their intended purpose, withdrawals from the Education Savings account follow a specific schedule:

  • Withdrawal Window: Funds are typically accessible only between **December and March** each year.
  • Withdrawal Process: Submit a completed Withdrawal Application Form to any NSLS branch during the open window.

About Us

Nambawan Savings & Loan Society (NSLS) is a newly established Savings & Loan Society under the Savings & Loans Societies (Amended) Act 1995. Nambawan Savings & Loan Society was established to serve the short term financial needs of the Nambawan Super Fund members.
