Dining & Entertainment

From gourmet dining at Reef 'n Beef to the friendly atmosphere of the Shamrock Pub, we offer the best culinary experiences in Boroko.

Reef 'n Beef Restaurant

Our premier in-house restaurant, open daily from 7:00 am to 9:30 pm. Enjoy a relaxed ambience while savoring a superb range of cuisine featuring the best local and international produce.

  • Hearty Breakfasts, Light Lunches, and Gourmet Dinners
  • Signature Guinness Hot Pots
  • Freshly prepared Gourmet Pizzas
  • Extensive Beverage Selection
  • Room Service available from the in-room menu

*Special meal and dietary items available by prior request.

The Shamrock Pub

The first Irish Pub in Port Moresby. A friendly place to meet friends and business associates. Relax, enjoy, and have a good time in an authentic pub setting.

Whether you're looking for a casual snack or a cold beverage, the Shamrock Pub and our Club Lounge provide the perfect backdrop for your evening.
