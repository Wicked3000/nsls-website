About Us

Nambawan Savings & Loan Society (NSLS)

What Is NSLS?

Nambawan Savings & Loan Society (NSLS) is a premier financial institution established under the Savings & Loans Societies (Amended) Act 1995, dedicated to empowering members through flexible savings and accessible credit.

Our Journey

Founded in 2010 to serve the short-term financial needs of Nambawan Super Limited (NSL) members, NSLS has evolved from a one-man desk into a nationwide force with over 42,000 members.

Today, we accept members from both public and private sectors, operating through 23 Nambawan Super Branches and 6 dedicated NSLS branches nationwide.

Who Can Join?

Membership is OPEN and voluntary for all contributing members of the Nambawan Super Fund. Our scheme provides the liquidity you need for life's important moments:

School Fees
Medical Expenses
Traditional Obligations
Urgent Commitments
