About Us

Nambawan Savings & Loan Society (NSLS)

What Is Nambawan Savings & Loan Society

Nambawan Savings & Loan Society (NSLS) is a newly established Savings & Loan Society under the Savings & Loans Societies (Amended) Act 1995.

Why was it setup?

Nambawan Savings and Loan Society was established in 2010 to serve the short term financial needs of Nambawan Super Limited (NSL) members only. From a one-man desk with less than a hundred members, the society now accepts members from both the public and private sector as per the Savings and Loan Act 2015.

From then, the society now grew to over 42,000 members. NSLS is located in 23 Nambawan Super Branches and in 6 NSLS branches nationwide.

Who is eligible to Join as a member of NSLS

Membership is OPEN to all contributing members of Nambawan Super Fund, and is voluntary. It is a voluntary savings scheme that gives you flexibility to access your savings to meet your urgent financial commitments such as:

  • Bride Price
  • School Fee
  • Funeral Expenses
  • Medical Expenses and so forth.
