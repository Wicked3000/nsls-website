Loan Products

Flexible credit solutions with the lowest interest rates in PNG.

Empowering Your Financial Potential

NSLS offers various loan ratios to suit your specific needs, whether you're borrowing against your savings or looking for greater financial flexibility.

1:1 Loan Ratio (Fully Secured)

  • Borrowing Power: Borrow up to the exact amount of your savings balance.
  • Low Interest: Competitive **1% per month** charged on the reducing balance.
  • Repayment: Flexible terms ranging from 3 to 36 months.

1:5 Loan Ratio (High Leverage)

  • Borrowing Power: Eligible members can borrow up to **five (5) times** their savings balance.
  • Interest: Highly affordable **1.5% per month** on the reducing balance.
  • Limits: Maximum loan of K100,000 (or K200,000 for authorized asset finance).

Eligibility Requirements

  • Saving History: Must have saved consistently for at least six (6) consecutive fortnights.
  • Minimum Balance: Maintaining a minimum of K200 in your General Savings account.
  • Documentation: Valid ID, recent pay slips, and a completed loan application form.

About Us

Nambawan Savings & Loan Society (NSLS) is a newly established Savings & Loan Society under the Savings & Loans Societies (Amended) Act 1995. Nambawan Savings & Loan Society was established to serve the short term financial needs of the Nambawan Super Fund members.
