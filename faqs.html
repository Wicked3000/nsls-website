General Frequently Asked Questions

Why should you open a savings account?
  • Funds are accessible immediately after three months of continuous contributions.
  • Beneficial interest rates and flexible contribution amounts.
  • Savings serve as security/collateral to obtain low-interest loans.
  • Access to the Nambawan Discount Program at various retailers.
What are the steps to opening a Savings & Loans Account?
  1. Obtain a membership application form from any NSLS office or Nambawan Super branch.
  2. Fill out the required details and provide valid identification.
  3. Complete an Irrevocable Authority to Deduct (IATD) form for payroll deductions.
  4. Submit both forms to your nearest NSLS or Nambawan Super office.
What are the rules for withdrawing savings?
  • Members can withdraw up to 50% of their net total savings in the General Savings account.
  • The minimum withdrawal amount is K200.00.
  • An exit fee of K20.00 applies per withdrawal.
  • Only one withdrawal is permitted per calendar month.
  • Funds are accessible after 3 months of continuous saving.
When Should I start contributing?

The best time to start is now! To ensure a successful savings journey, we recommend:

  • Setting a clear financial target and timeframe.
  • Creating a simple budget to protect your savings contributions.
  • Sticking consistently to your payroll deduction plan.
Who is eligible to join NSLS?

Any contributing member of Nambawan Super Fund is eligible to join NSLS. We also serve individuals with regular incomes, including SME owners and agriculture farmers.

What are the loan interest rates?

Our rates are among the most competitive in PNG:

  • 1:1 Ratio: 1% per month on the reducing balance (Fully secured).
  • 1:5 Ratio: 1.5% per month on the reducing balance (Leveraged).

About Us

Nambawan Savings & Loan Society (NSLS) is a newly established Savings & Loan Society under the Savings & Loans Societies (Amended) Act 1995. Nambawan Savings & Loan Society was established to serve the short term financial needs of the Nambawan Super Fund members.
